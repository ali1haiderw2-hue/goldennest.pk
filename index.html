How Golden Nest works

A simple overview of the proposed customer journey.

🐔

1. Buy a hen

Submit a purchase request for a hen at the listed price. The farm confirms payment and assignment.

🥚

2. Follow egg records

View recorded collection and allocation details. Daily output can vary and is not guaranteed.

↗

3. Manage resale

Submit a resale request. The farm verifies hen status and transfer conditions before completion.

Important: This is a free website prototype. No real purchase, ownership transfer, egg sale, wallet credit or payment is processed. Any earnings or payout terms must be agreed in a written customer contract and financially verified before launch.
